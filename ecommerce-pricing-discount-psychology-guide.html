The Psychology of Discounts: How to Run 20% Off Sales Without Killing Your Profit Margin

Running a flash 20% or 30% discount boosts top-line order volume immediately, but many store owners fail to calculate how deeply discounts eat into net profit per unit.

1. The Hidden Math of Discounting

Suppose you sell a product for $100 that costs you $60 to source and fulfill—leaving a $40 profit (40% margin). If you offer a 20% discount, your new selling price is $80, while your product cost remains $60. Your profit per unit drops from $40 to $20—meaning a 20% discount cut your actual profit in half! You must now sell 2x as many units just to make the exact same total profit.

2. Jonah Berger's "Rule of 100" for Promotions

  • Products under $100: Always use percentage discounts (e.g., "25% Off" on a $40 item sounds much bigger than "$10 Off").
  • Products over $100: Always use absolute dollar discounts (e.g., "$150 Off" on a $600 item converts better than "25% Off").
