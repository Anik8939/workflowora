📖 FINANCIAL & BUSINESS DICTIONARY

Searchable A-to-Z Glossary of Essential Finance & SaaS Terms

APR vs. APYAPR is the simple nominal annual interest rate without intra-year compounding; APY includes daily or monthly compounding.
ARPU / ARPAAverage Revenue Per User (or Account), typically measured monthly (MRR divided by total active customers).
Break-Even ROASThe minimum Return on Ad Spend multiplier where ad revenue covers product COGS and ad spend ($0 net profit): 1 ÷ Gross Margin %.
Burn Rate & Cash RunwayNet monthly negative cash flow (Burn Rate) and the number of months before cash reserves hit $0 (Cash Balance ÷ Net Burn).
CAC & LTV:CAC RatioCustomer Acquisition Cost compared against Customer Lifetime Value. A healthy SaaS or DTC business targets a 3.0:1+ LTV:CAC ratio.
CAGR (Compound Annual Growth)The smoothed annualized growth rate of an investment over multiple years: (Ending Value ÷ Beginning Value)^(1/Years) − 1.
DTI (Debt-to-Income Ratio)Total required monthly debt minimum payments divided by gross monthly pre-tax income. Lenders prefer ≤28% housing and ≤36% total DTI.
Gross Margin vs. MarkupGross Margin divides profit by selling price (P−C)/P; Markup divides profit by unit cost (P−C)/C. A 100% markup equals a 50% margin.
NRR (Net Revenue Retention)Percentage of recurring revenue retained from an existing customer cohort over 12 months including upgrades and subtracting churn.
