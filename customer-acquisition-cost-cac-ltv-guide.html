Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) & Lifetime Value (LTV): The 3:1 Golden Ratio Explained

Whether you run a subscription SaaS tool, an e-commerce brand, or a service agency, two numbers determine whether your business grows wealthier or goes broke as it scales: CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) and LTV (Customer Lifetime Value).

1. Simple Formulas for CAC and LTV

CAC = Total Marketing & Ad Spend ÷ Number of New Customers Acquired
LTV = Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × Gross Margin %

2. Interpreting Your LTV:CAC Ratio

  • Below 1.5 : 1 — You are losing money on acquisition once overhead is counted. Fix landing page conversion or raise average order value immediately.
  • 3.0 : 1 to 4.0 : 1 — The sweet spot for healthy, profitable scaling.
  • Above 5.0 : 1 — Strong unit economics; you can safely invest more into growth channels.
