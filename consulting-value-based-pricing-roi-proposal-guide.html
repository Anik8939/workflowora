3. Three Costly Calculation Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

When auditing real-world spreadsheets across startups, independent contractors, and household budgets, the same three mathematical blind spots appear repeatedly:

  • Ignoring Hidden Variable Costs & Processing Fees: Whether you are calculating e-commerce ROAS, SaaS CAC, or a mortgage refinance, omitting payment processing fees (2.9% + $0.30), shipping fulfillment, software overhead, or loan origination charges inflates your projected net return by 12% to 25%. Always enter fully loaded costs into the calculator above.
  • Confusing Simple Averages with Compounded Rates: A 2% monthly churn rate or a 22% credit card APR compounds every single billing cycle. Over 12 to 36 months, ignoring compounding frequency creates a massive gap between a back-of-the-napkin estimate and your actual bank balance.
  • Optimizing a Single Metric in Isolation: Lowering Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by attracting low-intent bargain hunters often spikes 60-day churn and destroys Lifetime Value (LTV). Similarly, choosing a 7-year loan over a 3-year loan lowers your monthly EMI by 35% but nearly doubles total interest paid. Always evaluate the ratio and total lifecycle cost together.
