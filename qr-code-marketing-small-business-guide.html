7 Smart Ways Small Businesses & Freelancers Use QR Codes to Win More Clients

Nobody enjoys typing a 35-character website link from a printed flyer or business card into a phone browser. Placing a high-contrast QR code bridges offline touchpoints directly to your digital portfolio, booking calendar, or review page in 2 seconds.

1. Seven High-Impact Places to Add a QR Code

  • On Printed & PDF Invoices: Link directly to your payment checkout page so clients can pay from their phone immediately.
  • Business Cards & Conference Badges: Point to your live portfolio or LinkedIn profile.
  • Product Packaging Inserts: Direct buyers to a 60-second setup guide or warranty registration page.
  • Google Review Cards: Let happy local customers scan once to leave a 5-star review.
