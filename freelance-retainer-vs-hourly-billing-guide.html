Monthly Retainers vs. Hourly Billing: How to Build Predictable Recurring Income

Relying solely on one-off projects forces you to start every month at $0 revenue, hunting for new leads. Converting just three satisfied clients onto a monthly retainer creates a predictable income floor that covers all your living expenses.

1. How to Structure a Win-Win Monthly Retainer

Never sell a retainer as "unlimited hours." Instead, package a fixed monthly deliverable outcome based on your calculated day rate—for example: 4 SEO Pillar Articles + Monthly Analytics Audit for $1,200/month, billed automatically on the 1st of every month via clean PDF invoice.
