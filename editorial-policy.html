Editorial Policy & Quantitative Calculation Standards

Last Verified: November 20, 2026 • WorkFlowora Editorial & Engineering Team

1. Our Editorial Commitment

WorkFlowora (www.workflowora.com) publishes interactive financial, SaaS, freelancing, tax, and productivity calculators paired with original long-form methodology guides. Because financial and tax topics directly affect real-world decisions, we hold every calculator engine and written guide to strict mathematical accuracy, plain-English clarity, and source transparency.

2. Formula Verification & Authoritative Sources

Before any calculator or guide goes live on WorkFlowora, its underlying equations are cross-checked against recognized industry and statutory references:

  • Loans, Mortgages & Consumer Credit: Reducing-balance amortization, Truth in Lending APR vs. Regulation DD APY conversions, and CFPB / Fannie Mae 28/36 and 43% DTI underwriting guidelines.
  • Tax & Self-Employment Estimators: IRS Schedule C, Schedule SE (92.35% net-earnings base, 15.3% FICA split, annual Social Security wage base, 50% above-the-line SE deduction), and Form 1040-ES quarterly schedules.
  • SaaS, E-Commerce & Startup Finance: Gross-margin-adjusted LTV:CAC, contribution-margin Break-Even ROAS, and cash-collected net burn / Default Alive modeling.
  • Productivity & Health Utilities: Peer-reviewed adult silent reading averages (238 WPM), conversational speaking pacing (120–150 WPM), WHO BMI classifications, and the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR/TDEE equation.

3. Strict Client-Side Calculation Privacy

All 14 calculators and the Global Tax Comparator run 100% inside your local browser via JavaScript. Your personal income, debt, tax deductions, or invoice data are never transmitted to WorkFlowora servers or stored in external databases.

4. Advertising Independence

WorkFlowora is supported by non-intrusive display advertising through Google AdSense so all tools and guides remain 100% free without paywalls or mandatory registration. Advertisers have zero influence over calculator formulas, tax benchmarks, editorial conclusions, or guide recommendations.

5. Updates & Corrections Policy

We review tax brackets, Social Security wage bases, and underwriting benchmarks annually and update both our calculator code and guides whenever statutory figures change. If you spot a calculation edge case, typo, or outdated reference, please email contact@workflowora.com. Verified corrections are deployed promptly with an updated verification date.
