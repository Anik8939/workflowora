SaaS Churn Rate & Net Revenue Retention (NRR): How Subscription Math Impacts LTV:CAC

In subscription businesses, memberships, and retainer agencies, Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) is controlled by one silent variable in the denominator: Churn Rate. Cutting your monthly churn in half literally doubles your LTV and doubles your LTV:CAC ratio without spending an extra dollar on marketing.

1. The Subscription LTV & Churn Formulas

Average Customer Lifespan (Months) = 1 ÷ Monthly Logo Churn Rate
Subscription LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin %) ÷ Monthly Churn Rate
Annual Churn Rate = 1 − (1 − Monthly Churn Rate)12

Worked Example: Suppose your SaaS charges $100/month (ARPU) with an 80% Gross Margin ($80 gross profit/month). If your monthly churn rate is 5% (0.05), your average customer stays for 1 ÷ 0.05 = 20 months, and your LTV is $1,600 ($80 ÷ 0.05). If you reduce monthly churn from 5% to 2.5%, average lifespan jumps to 40 months and your LTV doubles to $3,200!

2. Why 5% Monthly Churn Does NOT Equal 60% Annual Churn

Because churn compounds against a shrinking cohort each month, you must use the compound retention formula 1 − (1 − c)12:

3. Net Revenue Retention (NRR): Growing Without New Customers

Net Revenue Retention (NRR) measures how much recurring revenue you retain from an existing cohort after adding expansion upgrades and subtracting downgrades and churn: NRR = ((Starting MRR + Expansion MRR − Churned MRR) ÷ Starting MRR) × 100. Top-tier SaaS companies achieve 110% to 130% NRR, meaning their revenue grows every year even before counting new customer acquisition.
