How Many Words Is a 5, 10, or 20-Minute Speech? Presentation Pacing & WPM Chart

Writing a keynote presentation, wedding toast, YouTube video script, or university defense? The biggest mistake speakers make is estimating talk length by page count instead of spoken Words Per Minute (WPM). Because we speak roughly 45% slower than we read silently, a script that takes 5 minutes to read in your head will take nearly 9 minutes to deliver out loud on stage.

1. The Speaking Time Formula (Words Per Minute)

Total Speech Word Count = Target Speaking Minutes × Speaking Pace (WPM)
Estimated Speaking Minutes = Total Script Words ÷ 130 WPM (Standard Presentation Pace)
  • Slow / Formal Keynote Pace (110 WPM): Ideal for technical lectures, non-native audiences, or auditoriums with echo and slide transitions.
  • Conversational Presentation Pace (130 – 140 WPM): The gold standard used by top TED Talk speakers and news anchors.
  • Fast / Energetic YouTube & Podcast Pace (155 – 165 WPM): Best for tightly edited short-form videos, audiobooks, and upbeat product demos.
