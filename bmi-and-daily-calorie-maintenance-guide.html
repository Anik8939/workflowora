Understanding Your BMI & Daily Calorie Maintenance (TDEE): A Practical Health Guide

Maintaining high energy during long desk-work sessions starts with understanding two foundational health metrics: your Body Mass Index (BMI) and your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).

1. How BMI Is Calculated & Standard Categories

BMI measures weight relative to height squared: Weight (kg) ÷ [Height (m)]2.

  • Under 18.5: Underweight range
  • 18.5 to 24.9: Normal / Healthy weight range
  • 25.0 to 29.9: Overweight range
  • 30.0 and above: Obese range

2. How to Use Your Daily Maintenance Calories

Your maintenance calorie number is the approximate energy your body burns in 24 hours. To lose weight sustainably without losing focus at work, aim for a modest 300 to 500 kcal daily deficit rather than extreme crash diets.
