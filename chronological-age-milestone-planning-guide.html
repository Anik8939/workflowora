Why Exact Chronological Age Matters for Exams, Visas, and Financial Milestones

Many government civil service exams, university admissions, scholarship portals, and insurance policies require your exact chronological age down to the year, month, and day as of a specific cut-off date.

1. Why Manual Date Subtraction Often Goes Wrong

Because months vary between 28, 29, 30, and 31 days—and leap years occur every four years—subtracting dates by hand frequently creates a 1-to-3-day error that can disqualify an application near the cutoff boundary. Using an automated chronological age calculator ensures 100% calendar precision.
