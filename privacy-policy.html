Privacy Policy

Published by WorkFlowora Editorial Team Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Overview and Client-Side Calculation Privacy

At WorkFlowora (https://www.workflowora.com), protecting visitor privacy is built into our architecture. All 14 interactive calculators, the Printable PDF Invoice Generator, and the Global Tax Comparator execute exclusively in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Numerical inputs you type into our calculators (such as income, loan amounts, tax deductions, or invoice client details) are never transmitted to or stored on WorkFlowora servers.

2. Information Stored Locally in Your Browser

To keep your experience consistent across pages without requiring an account, WorkFlowora stores lightweight preference keys in your browser's localStorage:

  • wf_theme — remembers your Light or Dark Mode selection.
  • wf_lang — remembers your selected interface language (EN, DE, FR, ES, JA, or PT).
  • wf_currency — remembers your preferred display currency.
  • wf_feature_requests & wf_voted_ids — stores your local feature-request submissions and upvotes on the Visitor Feature Request Board.

You can clear these keys at any time through your browser settings.

3. Advertising, Google AdSense, and Third-Party Cookies

WorkFlowora uses Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-8731058976498303) to display advertisements that keep our calculators and guides free to use. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to WorkFlowora or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • You can also review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Analytics and Tag Management

We use Google Tag Manager (GTM-TSRSH3VS) to manage analytics tags and measure aggregated site performance (such as page views, broken links, and device types) so we can improve site speed and content relevance. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. GDPR (EEA/UK) and CCPA/CPRA (California) Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you have voluntarily sent to us (for example, via email to contact@workflowora.com), as well as the right to opt out of personalized advertising cookies.

6. Children's Privacy

WorkFlowora is intended for a general adult audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA).

7. Contact for Privacy Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, email contact@workflowora.com.
