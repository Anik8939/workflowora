Profit Margin vs. Markup & Break-Even ROAS: The Complete Business Math Guide

Confusing markup with profit margin—or scaling paid ads without knowing your break-even ROAS—is the fastest way for an online store or digital business to lose money even while sales look high.

1. Profit Margin vs. Markup: What Is the Difference?

  • Gross Profit Margin (%): Shows what percentage of the final selling price is profit. Formula: (Selling Price − Cost) ÷ Selling Price × 100.
  • Markup (%): Shows how much you added above your product cost. Formula: (Selling Price − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100.

For example, if a product costs $35 to make and you sell it for $95, your gross profit before ads is $60. Once you subtract a $20 customer acquisition ad cost, your net profit is $40 per order—giving you a healthy 42.11% net profit margin.

2. How to Calculate Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

Campaign ROAS = Total Revenue Generated From Ads ÷ Total Ad Spend

If you spend $20 on Google or Meta ads to generate a $95 sale, your ROAS is $95 ÷ $20 = 4.75x. To find your Break-Even ROAS, divide 1 by your pre-ad gross margin percentage. Any campaign performing above your break-even ROAS generates net profit.
